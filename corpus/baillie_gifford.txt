Baillie Gifford is a large-scale investment business that has remained an independent private partnership. Founded in 1908, we are one of the UK's oldest independent investment management firms. We believe we are unique for a company of our size, being an independent partnership with no outside shareholders and unlimited liability to our clients, for whom we invest 213 billion pounds. Our single focus is on providing outstanding investment returns and service for those clients. We measure success by the value we add for clients after fees, not by our own revenues. We firmly believe that if we deliver for clients, we can be confident about our own future.
We are long-term active investors, not speculators. Our investment philosophy focuses on growth. We are as enthusiastic now as we were at the beginning about uncovering and investing in the world's most innovative companies. As long-term owners of our own business, we believe we have a culture which differentiates us from others, allowing us to take a very long-term view on everything that we do.
As our record of organic growth has continued, the firm has steadily diversified both in terms of client base and the range of investment strategies we offer. This includes seven of the largest twenty pension funds in the world, as well as financial institutions, sovereign wealth funds, endowments and foundations, family offices and individuals.
