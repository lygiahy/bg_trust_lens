Let's start at the beginning, what actually is investing? Well, it's when you use your money to buy things that you hope will increase in value over time. If they do, you can sell them down the line for a profit. The things that you can buy are called assets in the financial world. Some of the big ones include stocks, funds, and bonds.
The most important thing to do before you start investing is making sure you have an emergency fund in place. This should cover your regular outgoings for three months minimum. Think of your emergency fund as the first step on your personal finance journey.
